Installation / service technician

Service and installation

We are looking for an installation and service technician to handle the installation, maintenance and servicing of our equipment directly at customer sites.

The role

  • Assembly and installation of our equipment and systems
  • Regular maintenance and servicing of equipment
  • Fault diagnosis and repair
  • Repair and replacement of damaged parts
  • Communication with customers and technical support
  • Training installation crews and keeping records
  • Compliance with safety regulations

Requirements

  • A category B driving licence (E an advantage)
  • Technical thinking and problem-solving ability
  • Manual skill, precision, independence and reliability
  • Experience of installing and servicing similar equipment
  • Working with a range of tools and equipment
  • Knowledge of electrical diagrams and the ability to read technical documentation
  • Flexibility and a willingness to travel
  • Good communication skills and the ability to deal with customers

What we offer

  • Stable employment with an established company
  • Motivating pay
  • Opportunities for professional growth and training
  • Meal and holiday allowances and other benefits
  • A pleasant working environment and teamwork
  • A mobile phone and modern technical facilities
