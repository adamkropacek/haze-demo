Projects with subsidy support.

HAZE s.r.o. carries out projects with the support of the European Union and the state budget of the Czech Republic. Below you will find an overview of current and completed projects, their registration numbers and descriptions.

Funded by the European UnionNextGenerationEU

Photovoltaic power plant without storage HAZE, s.r.o.

Registration number
CZ.31.3.0/0.0/0.0/22_001/0005948

Project description: The project covers the construction of a photovoltaic power plant with an installed capacity of 98.4 kWp, without storage, on the building (building plot no. 101, cadastral area 76180) at the production premises of HAZE, s.r.o. in the village of Křičeň, Pardubice Region.

Project objectives: Through the use of renewable sources the project will reduce emissions of pollutants and lower the company's operating costs.

The project is co-financed from European Union funds - Construction of new photovoltaic sources; 2.3 Transition to clean energy sources.

European UnionEuropean Regional Development Fund Ministry of Industry and Trade

Digitalisation and automation of production at HAZE, s. r. o.

Our company is carrying out the project "Digitalisation and automation of production at HAZE, s. r. o.", which is co-financed by the Operational Programme Enterprise and Innovation for Competitiveness, that is from European Union funds and the state budget of the Czech Republic. The project was carried out with financial support from the state budget through the Ministry of Industry and Trade.

The main objective of the project is to extend the existing interconnection of technologies to further machines, areas and applications that have not yet been fully digitalised and included in data collection. The output of the project is deeper two-way communication between the individual elements.

European UnionEuropean Regional Development Fund Ministry of Industry and Trade

Construction of the production premises of HAZE, s. r. o.

Our company is carrying out the project "Construction of the production premises of HAZE, s. r. o.", which is co-financed by the Operational Programme Enterprise and Innovation for Competitiveness, that is from European Union funds and the state budget of the Czech Republic.

The objective of the project is to bring the production, storage and administrative areas of HAZE, s. r. o. together in one place. The creation of the new premises will improve the facilities for employees, and the new premises will also better reflect and strengthen the company's position on the market, thereby increasing its competitiveness.

Ministry of Industry and Trade

B2B product configurator for HAZE

Registration number
FX05030153_7929
Beneficiary
HAZE s.r.o.

Project description: The project plan will enable HAZE, s.r.o. to increase its volume of output significantly, above all abroad, thanks to the automation of quotation, pricing and ordering activities by means of a product configurator.

The project was carried out with financial support from the state budget through the Ministry of Industry and Trade under The Country for the Future programme.

We manufacture and install gates, side ventilation, curtain partition walls and accessories for modern livestock buildings and industry. Since 2007, with our own plant in Křičeň and completed projects in CZ, SK and abroad.
