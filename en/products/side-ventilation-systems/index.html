Side ventilation systems.

A side ventilation system allows the supply of fresh air to be adjusted steplessly by opening the side wall of the livestock building partly or fully. Combined with ridge ventilation it creates the conditions for effective natural ventilation, helps to maintain a stable microclimate and ensures sufficient air exchange all year round. An optimum microclimate contributes to greater animal comfort, better herd health and more even intake of feed and water, and creates the conditions for a higher milk yield. Operation can be manual, using a winch, or electric. The electric version can be linked to a HAZE weather station, which automatically controls how far the system opens according to the current weather conditions.

Types A to F in detail

Every type explained.

Vysoké Studnice project
A
Roll-up curtain wall

Type A side ventilation system

A proven solution whose performance has been confirmed by more than 20 years in service. At the top the curtain is suspended from steel cables connected to the main cable, which winds over guide pulleys onto the drum at the manual winch - this is what moves the curtain vertically. At the bottom it is fixed firmly to the sill board and folds onto the sill as it moves. A support net holds the curtain in place in adverse weather.

PrincipleWinds up at the top, folds onto the sill at the bottom
OperationManual winch
InfillPVC curtain resistant to UV and frost
Max. size65 x 3.5 m (length x height)
Okrouhlá Radouň project
B
Roll-up curtain wall

Type B side ventilation system

One of the most popular side ventilation solutions. Compared with type A, a shaft connected to the motor is threaded through the curtain at the bottom - the curtain winds up at the bottom, so it does not get dirty and stays permanently taut whether fully or partly closed. It is powered by an electric drive, which can be connected to a HAZE weather station so that the wall is controlled automatically.

PrincipleWinds up, mounted on the bottom shaft
OperationElectric drive
InfillPVC curtain resistant to UV and frost
Max. size67 x 3 m (length x height)
Telecí project
C
Roller curtain wall · versions C / C+

Type C side ventilation system

The curtain is fixed firmly to the top aluminium strip and, as it opens, it unrolls downwards onto the lower "sill", which gives stepless adjustment of how far the side wall is open. The system is driven by an electric drive and, thanks to the side guide profiles, it withstands wind loads even on larger openings. It allows a high intensity of natural ventilation and is therefore particularly suitable for bull barns, riding arenas and livestock buildings with a higher concrete plinth, which restricts direct air flow into the area of the animals' heads and nostrils. For higher structural openings we supply the C+ version with two winding shafts; the final solution is always designed for the specific building.

PrincipleUnrolls downwards, mounted on the top aluminium strip
OperationElectric drive
InfillPVC curtain resistant to UV and frost
Max. size, version C100 x 4 m (length x height)
Max. size, version C+60 x 5 m (length x height)
Příkazy project
D
Roller curtain wall · versions D / D+

Type D side ventilation system

The curtain rolls onto a bottom shaft driven by an electric drive, and the top fixing is movable as well, controlled by a second motor. The wall therefore provides both ventilation and shading. Instead of support nets it uses heavy-duty profiles fitted with rubber. Central curtain sealing between the walls prevents air from blowing through, and edge curtain sealing protects the edges against tearing in the wind and against physical damage. For higher openings we choose the D+ version with two shafts; the final solution is always designed for the specific building.

PrincipleRolls onto the bottom shaft + movable top fixing (2 motors)
OperationElectric drive
InfillPVC curtain resistant to UV and frost
Max. size100 x 4 m (length x height)
Max. size, version D+60 x 6 m (length x height)
Lysá nad Labem project
E
Roller curtain wall

Type E side ventilation system

Designed for agricultural and industrial buildings with high demands on reliability and durability. The curtain is fixed in the top aluminium strip and unrolls downwards from it. In the middle the curtain has a keder bead for fixing into the winding shaft - as a result it winds twice as fast as conventional systems and responds quickly to changes in the microclimate. The bottom shaft keeps the curtain taut and a welded-on collar seals the gap down to the ground. Suitable for very wide entrances. It can be manufactured in any shade from the colour chart.

PrincipleUnrolls downwards, mounted on the top aluminium strip + winding shaft in the middle - unrolls 2x faster
OperationElectric drive
InfillPVC curtain resistant to UV and frost
Max. size60 x 5 m (length x height)
Bohdalov project
F
Polycarbonate wall

Type F side ventilation system

The infill is 32 mm multiwall polycarbonate. We recommend this version for buildings with higher demands on thermal insulation, in particular for collecting yards in front of milking parlours. We supply the polycarbonate sheets in various grades of light transmission, so they bring plenty of natural daylight into the building. Available in 2 variants - a single-section or a two-section wall. The system can be operated by a manual winch or by an electric drive.

PrinciplePolycarbonate sheets wind onto the top pulley
OperationManual winch / Electric drive
Infill32 mm polycarbonate sheet
Version1-section / 2-section
Max. sizeDetermined individually

Frequently asked questions

What you ask most often.

What is the wall infill made of?

On types A to E it is a PVC curtain resistant to UV radiation and frost; on type F it is 32 mm multiwall polycarbonate. We choose polycarbonate where better thermal insulation and daylight transmission are needed.

How is the side wall operated?

By a manual winch (by hand) or by an electric drive. The electric types can be connected to a HAZE weather station and the wall then controlled automatically according to temperature and wind.

How large a wall can you cover?

It depends on the type of system. Type A can cover up to 65 x 3.5 m, type B 67 x 3 m, types C and D up to 100 x 4 m (versions C+ and D+ for higher openings 60 x 5 and 60 x 6 m respectively) and type E 60 x 5 m. For the polycarbonate type F the size is determined individually. The final configuration is always verified during the on-site survey.

Can the wall provide shading as well, not just ventilation?

Yes, type D has movable top and bottom fixings and provides both ventilation and shading at the same time.

How does installation work?

We manufacture the wall made to measure and our own installation team carries out the installation. After handover we train the operators. We provide a 2-year warranty on the technology and the installation.

Can the wall replace roller gates?

On wide entrances yes - thanks to its robust structure and variable sizes, type E in particular makes this possible.
