What we manufacture

Everything made to measure, manufactured in our own plant.

01.

Roller gates

Electric, insulated, manual and chain-operated. For livestock buildings, storage buildings and shelters. Space-saving, quiet running, long service life.

02.

Side ventilation systems

Side ventilation system types A-F. Roll-up, roller and polycarbonate walls. Insulation in winter, air exchange in summer - with no draughts on the animals.

Product details →
03.

Ridge vents

Ridge vents without a damper and double-leaf versions. Free extraction of warm air working together with side ventilation - proven welfare for cattle farming.

04.

Other gates

Hinged gates, sliding gates and passages for livestock buildings, industrial buildings and stores. A draught stop and a robust structure made to measure for the entrance.

Weather station

Measurement of temperature, humidity and wind directly at the livestock building. The basis for ventilation control and herd comfort all year round.

Slurry tank roofing

How we work together

From consultation to completed project in 4-10 weeks.

01. Consultation

Survey and enquiry

We call you or come to the site. We measure the actual dimensions.

02. Quotation

Proposal within 48 hours

A specific price, materials and delivery date. No hidden items.

03. Manufacture

Our own plant in Křičeň

We manufacture in-house across 2,500 m². We control the quality of every weld and every profile.

04. Installation

Installation by our own team

Installation is carried out by our own team. After handover we train the operators.
