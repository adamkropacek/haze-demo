Nets for ventilation.

Ventilation nets form the breathable infill of side ventilation systems and cover wall areas of the livestock building. We offer three variants - the SCRIM anti-draught net, the H08 net and the H25 net. They differ in air permeability, dimensions and application. Below you will find details of each variant and a comparison.

Variants in detail

Every net explained.

Against draughts

SCRIM anti-draught net

A dense fabric with low air permeability that protects against direct draughts. It is used to cover wall areas, above all gable areas, and also the sides of riding arenas. We supply it in rolls of 3 × 100 m.

Net infills

Instead of a PVC curtain, the sides of a building can be fitted with a fixed infill, as standard a SCRIM net in the colours of the colour chart. The net is permanently air-permeable, prevents bird ingress and reduces draughts on the animals. It suits sites where the wall is to ventilate continuously. It is normally installed in riding arenas, where constant air permeability is desirable. We choose the specific net according to the required permeability - see the variants above.

Frequently asked questions

What you ask most often.

What does open area mean?

It states the proportion of the net that is open. The H25 support net lets through the maximum amount of air and serves as the infill of type A ventilation systems, H08 has medium permeability for covering smaller areas, and the dense SCRIM anti-draught net protects against direct draughts.

Which net goes with a side ventilation system?

The H25 support net is used with type A side systems. We will help you choose according to the specific version.

What is the SCRIM anti-draught net suitable for?

For protection against direct draughts - to cover wall areas, above all gable areas, and also the sides of riding arenas. It is a dense fabric with low air permeability, supplied in rolls of 3 × 100 m.

Which colours do you supply the nets in?

We supply the SCRIM anti-draught net in black and grey, H08 in green and H25 in black.

Do you supply nets with the ventilation installation as well?

Yes. The nets are part of our side and ridge ventilation systems and we supply them together with the structure and the installation. They can also be ordered separately.

What size rolls do you supply?

It depends on the variant - H25 2 × 35 m, H08 2 × 50 m, SCRIM anti-draught net 3 × 100 m. We calculate the quantity needed from the area.
