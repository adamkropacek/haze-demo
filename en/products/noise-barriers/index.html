Noise barriers made to measure.

A steel structure designed to limit the spread of noise and to divide the interior of a building. It is suitable for sports halls, sports grounds, municipalities and industrial sites - wherever operating noise has to be reduced or one large space has to be divided into separate parts with different uses. We manufacture the structure at our own plant and installation is carried out by our own team, so we keep control of quality, deadlines and dimensions.

Request a quote within 48h
Krpálek Academy, Praha-Hloubětín - 240 m² partition wall
Structure and specifications

How the wall is built.

The load-bearing structure is a steel frame with a hot-dip galvanised or powder-coated finish. The standard infill is tarpaulin fabric in a colour from the colour chart. At the customer's request a different type of tarpaulin fabric can be chosen to suit the requirements of the specific site. We design every wall to the specific brief and, because we manufacture and install it ourselves, we keep control of quality, dimensions and delivery dates. If necessary, the infill modules can be replaced without dismantling the load-bearing structure.

Who it is for

Where the wall solves the problem.

We design the wall to suit the situation - from a sports hall through an industrial site to dividing an interior space.

  • Sports hallNoise limits.Operating noise runs up against the limits towards neighbouring buildings. Our structure allows operation without restricted hours.
  • Industrial siteNeighbouring buildings.Noise from manufacturing or logistics disturbs a residential zone. We build the modular wall without long shutdowns of the operation.
  • Partition wallsDividing the space.Partition and screening walls made of PVC curtain or of solid panels, for example the 240 m² partition wall in Lukáš Krpálek's sports hall.

Frequently asked questions

What you ask most often.

What is the wall built from?

The load-bearing element is a steel frame, hot-dip galvanised or powder-coated. The standard infill is tarpaulin fabric in a colour from the colour chart; on request a different type of tarpaulin fabric can be chosen to suit the requirements of the site.

How large a wall can you build?

The system is modular, with a standard height of 2 to 6 m and no limit on length. On longer runs we design expansion joints according to the actual length.

Do you also build ordinary partition walls?

Yes. Besides noise barriers we also manufacture partition and screening walls made of PVC curtain and of solid panels. One example is the 240 m² partition wall in Lukáš Krpálek's sports hall.

Who manufactures and installs the wall?

We manufacture in our own plant and install with our own team. This means we keep control of the dimensions and the delivery date and we are not dependent on subcontractors.

We manufacture and install gates, side ventilation, noise barriers and accessories for modern livestock buildings and for industry. Operating since 2007, with our own production plant in Křičeň and completed projects in the Czech Republic, Slovakia and abroad.
