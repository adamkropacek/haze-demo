Why it matters

The indoor climate of a livestock building costs money every day. In feed, in output and in repairs.

Heat, humidity, draughts and leaks do not show all at once - first animal comfort falls, then feed intake, and finally the productivity of the herd. Our solutions keep conditions in the building stable all year round.

20-22°C

Heat counts before the building feels hot

In dairy cows heat stress begins at around 20-22 °C. The cows then eat less and milk yield falls. Ventilation removes heat and moisture without creating a draught on the animals.

48 h

First the survey, then a specific proposal

We measure the dimensions, the orientation of the building and the places where it draughts or leaks. We then prepare the price, materials and schedule within two working days.

365 days

In winter it is mainly about humidity

Even a closed building has to ventilate in winter. It removes moisture, dust and gases and protects the structure against condensation, without blowing directly on the animals.

2 years

Warranty on the technology and the installation

We manufacture the solution in our own plant and our own team installs it. The warranty covers the technology and the installation to the agreed scope.

About HAZE

Since 2007 we have been building livestock buildings that stand up to daily use.

We started out as a metalworking shop. Today we have 2 500 m² of production space, our own design team and more than 10 000 completed projects behind us in the Czech Republic, Slovakia and abroad.

Every livestock building is different - there is no catalogue size and no universal solution. Every operation has different air flow, different loading on the gates, a specific operating routine and its own weak points.

That is why, since 2007, HAZE has not supplied ready-made templates but technical solutions designed to measure. We survey every building, design a suitable structure, manufacture it in our own plant and install it with our own team.
