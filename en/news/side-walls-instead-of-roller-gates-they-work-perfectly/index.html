Completed project29 July 2020

Side walls instead of roller gates? They work perfectly!

How do you deal with a situation where the customer wants roller gates wider than can be manufactured? During the construction of the premises of STROM PRAHA, the exclusive distributor of John Deere machinery for the Czech Republic, we solved it simply - instead of roller gates we recommended a type E side ventilation system to the client. And - as you can see for yourself in the photographs - it works brilliantly!

The opening height is 3.5 m in all cases; the curtains were then installed in four widths of 14.9 (two units), 17, 20 and 14.5 linear metres. All visible components are finished in anthracite so that the overall appearance of the building is preserved. In the space beneath the rolled-up curtain there are two vertical sliding guide supports, which provide the maximum drive-through opening. Without moving the vertical supports, a drive-through width of 5-7 linear metres is always maintained, depending on the width of the opening in question. We also installed a fixed curtain infill above the opening and at the side.

"This top contract was 12 months in the making. We were involved in the project from the very beginning and were there when every detail was chosen," says Petr Hanousek, managing director of Haze, commenting on the build. "It was already our second collaboration with our VIP client STROM PRAHA, and a third one is coming up this year. The customer is satisfied with our work, and that is the most important thing for us."
