Industrial project: ventilation system (version E)

Our ventilation technology is finding further applications. We have used it outside agriculture - in an industrial building in Lysá nad Labem. This project is an important step for us. Effective ventilation and light control are key not only in livestock buildings, but also in production and storage areas.

The main element of the solution is an electric roller curtain side wall (version E), which allows flexible control of the air flow. Thanks to its rigid structure and durable curtain infill it protects the interior against adverse conditions, while the maintenance-free drive makes operation simple.

Correct ventilation in industrial buildings affects more than just comfort. It helps to maintain optimum conditions for workers and equipment, reduces heating and cooling costs and contributes to energy savings. Natural ventilation also limits the build-up of dust and harmful substances. This improves the protection of employees' health.

This completed project marks an expansion of our activities into a new sector. Our technology is just as beneficial in an industrial environment as in agriculture. In future we therefore want to take part in further projects that bring efficient and sustainable solutions to production facilities and storage areas.
