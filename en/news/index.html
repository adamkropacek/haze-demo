What happens next

1

We call within 24 hours

A short phone call - we will clarify the scope and agree a date for the on-site survey.

2

On-site survey

We will come to you, physically measure the dimensions and look at the overall context of the livestock building.

3

A firm quotation within 48 hours

Price, materials, schedule. No hidden items - what you sign is what applies.

4

Manufacture and installation in 4-10 weeks

Manufacture in our own plant, installation at the project site. After handover we will train the operators and issue the warranty.
