Completed project28 November 2020

One of the most interesting projects of 2020? The Bohdalov farm!

Although we completed dozens of fine projects in 2020, the Bohdalov farm is definitely one of the most interesting. Two brand-new cattle barns were built here, including a collecting yard and a milking parlour, and we supplied a whole range of technology components for them.

In the case of the livestock buildings, ventilation of the new structures is handled by our most advanced side ventilation system - the type D ventilation system. In this model the curtain rolls onto the shaft to the required height, and the system can also move vertically. This solution therefore provides not only ventilation but also shading. The side ventilation systems are complemented by GRP ridge vents without control.

For the collecting yard building the owners then chose a type F ventilation system, which is made up of polycarbonate walls. This material is ideally suited to collecting yards and milking parlours, that is, places where greater thermal insulation is required. The collecting yard and the milking parlour are separated by hinged and sliding gates made of PUR panels; in the milking parlour we installed a double-leaf ridge vent and roller gates. You will also find Haze roller gates in the farm buildings at Bohdalov.
