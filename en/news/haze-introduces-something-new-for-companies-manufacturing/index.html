Partnerships19 May 2022

Haze introduces something new for companies - manufacturing partnerships!

The past two years have been pivotal for Haze - first we built the new plant in Křičeň, then we equipped it with several modern machines that we use to manufacture our products. These machines are now available to our partner companies as well - so that they do not have to buy expensive equipment themselves, we are happy to process their products on ours.

To produce the curtains we use in our side ventilation systems and roller gates, we now use a TARPA PRO high-frequency welder from Zemat Technology Group. The ZDWA-20-C-3 model can weld two or more layers of plastics, mainly polyvinyl chloride (PVC), polyurethane, polyamide, polyester and other derivatives of these compounds. The main advantage is the very high quality of the linear weld. With this technology the weld is as strong as the original material, which means greater strength of the finished product.

For machining timber beams and other wooden building components we now use a fully automatic four-sided WEINIG Cube Plus planer. It is ten times faster than surface and thickness planers and produces perfectly square-edged components.

Metalworking is now made easier by three machines - hydraulic shears, a hydraulic press brake and a band saw. The DURMAZLAR VS 3006 hydraulic guillotine shears are designed for cutting sheet material (steel sheet, aluminium, stainless steel) in several widths and quantities. We use the DURMAZLAR AD-R 30175 hydraulic press brake for bending sheet metal of various thicknesses and grades. And the automatic FMB JUPITER+CN band saw is widely used in our production for cutting metal sections and drawn metal materials.

We are not yet using all of these machines to 100 % of their capacity, so we can offer part of that capacity to our partner companies as well. You will find more information in the Partnerships section. If you are interested in working with us, please contact our production manager Roman Hátle at hatle@haze.cz.
