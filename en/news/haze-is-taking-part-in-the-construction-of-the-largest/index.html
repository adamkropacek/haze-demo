Completed projects24 August 2020

Haze is helping to build the largest livestock building in Europe

It is being built in Nové Zámky in Slovakia and will be the largest structure of its kind in Europe - it will be 300 metres long and will house up to 1600 dairy cows. The build is currently half finished; the first part of the cattle barn is 150 metres long and holds 800 dairy cows. Our company supplied type D side ventilation walls for this livestock building, currently our most advanced side ventilation system, which is also connected to an AGP 05 weather station.

The livestock building, measuring 65 x 300 metres, will be an exceptional structure. Not only because of its size, but also because the plot it stands on is on a slope, something our installed side ventilation system (version D) had to cope with as well. One reason the owners chose this side ventilation system is that Nové Zámky lies in a lowland area where it is very windy. They needed a solution that would cope best with these conditions and that would give the cattle maximum comfort and the staff easy operation.

With the type D side wall the curtain rolls up to the required height and also allows vertical adjustment, which makes it the most suitable solution in terms of shading. "The client also chose the side ventilation system (version D) because it met the height parameters and offers a stronger structure," says Petr Hanousek, owner of Haze.

On each side of the building the fitters installed a total of 4 roller curtains - each of them 75 metres long and 3.5 metres high. With our type D side ventilation system the maximum length of a single wall is determined by its height. In general, we can say that the optimum length of a single unit is 60 metres at heights of up to 4 metres - given that livestock buildings have cattle crossover alleys, in most cases 60 metres is the maximum length.

In the livestock building at Nové Zámky the roller curtains are connected to an AGP 05 weather station. It responds to changing weather conditions and, according to preset values, automatically adjusts the ventilation system so that it maintains an optimal microclimate in the building. The curtains on the two sides of the structure can move independently of each other. Our fitters therefore installed wind, temperature and rain sensors on each side of the building. All the data from the sensors is then collected and evaluated in the weather station. With the AGP 05 type, both the settings and the monitoring itself can be carried out either from the main panel or from a computer or tablet via the web interface.

Installation of the type D side ventilation systems in Nové Zámky began at the start of March and took several weeks. Connecting and commissioning the whole system took about three days. The entire livestock building will be completed in 2021.
