Olbramice-Klimkovice, Ostrava

RenoFarma Slezan - Olbramice cattle barn

In 2020 we took part in the construction of one of the most modern cattle barns in the Czech Republic, built by RenoFarmy on its farm in Olbramice. It features the best products in the Haze range - a double-leaf ridge vent operated by an electric drive, a type D side ventilation system and electric roller gates. Most operations on the farm are fully automated, and that applies to our components too - all of them are controlled by the latest model of the Haze weather station. Besides the ridge vent, the side curtains and the roller gates (finished in the client's corporate colours at his request), we also supplied several smaller items for the project, for example fixed wall infills and milking parlour equipment.

Ovčáry u Kolína

John Deere Ovčáry

For the construction of its premises in Ovčáry u Kolína, our VIP customer STROM PRAHA, the exclusive distributor of John Deere machinery for the Czech Republic, wanted roller gates in a wider version than can be manufactured. Instead we recommended a type E side ventilation system. The opening height is 3.5 m and the curtains are 14.9 linear m, 17 linear m, 20 linear m and 14.5 linear m wide. All visible components are finished in anthracite so that the overall appearance of the building is preserved. In the space beneath the rolled-up curtain there are two vertical sliding guide supports, which allow the maximum drive-through opening. Without moving the vertical supports, a drive-through width of 5-7 linear m is always maintained, depending on the width of the opening. We also installed a fixed curtain infill above the opening and at the side.

Okrouhlá Radouň, South Bohemian Region

Okrouhlá Radouň farm

In the new, wonderfully spacious and light-filled cattle barn at the agricultural cooperative in Okrouhlá Radouň we installed a type B side ventilation system. Today it is one of the most popular systems. Here the curtain is suspended at the top from steel cables, which are connected to the main cable that winds over guide pulleys onto the drum at the motor. This is what provides the vertical movement. At the bottom, a shaft connected to the motor is threaded through the curtain. As the curtain moves, it is wound up at the same time at the bottom of the opening. The ventilation system is connected to the latest Haze weather station. At Okrouhlá Radouň you will also find our roller gates and sliding gates with tongue-and-groove board infill. In the roof there is a non-adjustable ridge vent.

Lysá nad Labem

H.A.N.S. Prefa

In an industrial building in Lysá nad Labem we installed an electric BVS E roller curtain wall, which provides effective ventilation and shading. The wall, up to 5 metres high, is fitted with a heavy-duty winding shaft and a maintenance-free electric drive with a torque from 120 Nm. The curtain infill with a weight of 690 g/m² helps to control the light conditions and at the same time stands up to the elements. The installation also included fixed side infills and protective curtain covers, which increase the functionality of the whole system. With this project we applied our technology in the industrial sector and so broadened the scope of our work.

Praha

Krpálek Academy

We took part in the modernisation of the Krpalek Academy sports hall in Praha, founded by Olympic champion Lukáš Krpálek. The academy focuses on developing a new generation of athletes and provides facilities for both professional and recreational training. For this project we supplied a two-layer partition wall made of PVC film, with an area of over 240 m² and a motorised winding system. It allows the hall to be divided into two separate sectors in moments. Athletes can therefore train in parallel, and event organisers will appreciate the flexibility when planning the programme. The wall also damps noise effectively, which adds to comfort and helps concentration.

Příkazy u Olomouce

ZD Unčovice - Příkazy site

In the calf barn at Unčovice we installed a ridge vent with a GRP arch and electric BVS D+ roller curtain walls up to 4.5 metres high. This system provides natural ventilation of the livestock building and allows flexible control of the air flow. The structure is made of hot-dip galvanised steel with GRP components that stand up to the weather. The delivery also included timber boarding to stabilise the walls, electric drives and protective covers. This solution significantly improves the climate in the building, which contributes to the comfort and health of the calves.

Bohdalov, Vysočina

Bohdalov farm

Bohdalov Farm is one of our long-standing clients. This time we took part in the construction of a new calf barn. In the building we installed a total of 6 type D+ side ventilation walls fitted with a collar that seals the bottom of the curtains. Nový Telečkov, Vysočina

Nový Telečkov farm

The calf barn for 210 calves in Nový Telečkov was approved for use in September 2019. Our company installed a type B side ventilation system (BVS B) in the building. In this system the curtain is suspended at the top from steel cables, which are connected to the main cable that winds over guide pulleys onto the drum at the motor. At the bottom, a shaft connected to the motor is threaded through the curtain. As the curtain moves, it is wound up at the same time at the bottom of the opening. The ventilation system is connected to a brand new Haze AG06 weather station with a modern touchscreen display, thanks to which the ventilation is controlled automatically. In the building you will also find a ridge vent and Haze electric roller gates.

Riding arena · ventilation7 photos
Holešov-Dobrotice, Zlín Region

Holešov-Dobrotice riding arena

The riding arena in Holešov-Dobrotice is operated by the company Jacom. The building used to have a solid wall, which made it impossible to ventilate the arena, and in summer the air inside was unbreathable. Haze replaced the solid wall with a type F side ventilation system, which consists of a polycarbonate wall. The solution is unique in that it is a single piece of polycarbonate wall (side walls are usually made up of several pieces), which can be lowered as far as required, so the arena can be ventilated as needed. We can manufacture a type F side ventilation system formed by a single wall up to 65 metres long.
